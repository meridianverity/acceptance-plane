# Glossary

## Acceptance Plane

The Acceptance Plane is an architectural function that determines whether a specific autonomous AI action should be accepted by a protected system before impact, based on current, scope-bound, verifier-ready evidence at the acceptance boundary.

## Acceptance Boundary

The point at which a proposed autonomous AI action may cross from intention or recommendation into protected-system consequence.

## Gate-Time Evidence

Current evidence evaluated at the acceptance boundary before a high-consequence action becomes real.

## Verifier-Ready Receipt

A structured record that shows the action attempted, authority/scope evaluated, evidence checked, policy state used, decision made, and reason for accepting, holding, or refusing.

## ACCEPT

The action may proceed because the evidence is current, scoped, consistent, and sufficient.

## HOLD

The action pauses because evidence is incomplete, stale, ambiguous, high-risk, or requires step-up review.

## REFUSE

The action is blocked because authority, scope, target, freshness, revocation, replay, or safety conditions fail.

## Fail-Closed Autonomy

An operating posture where autonomous actions proceed only when required proof is sufficient and are held or refused when proof is incomplete or fails.

## Protected System

A system, workflow, environment, dataset, infrastructure boundary, or operational process whose state or data should not be changed by autonomous action without evidence-bound acceptance.
